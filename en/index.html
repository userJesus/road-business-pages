What is CAPITALIST?

CAPITALIST is a free online board game of strategy and property management featuring Brazilian states and beaches. Buy properties, build houses and hotels, and collect rent. Play with 2–4 players online or solo against the BOT, right in your browser.

Is it similar to Monopoly or Banco Imobiliário?

Yes. If you enjoy rolling dice, buying land and collecting rent in games such as Monopoly and Banco Imobiliário, you will recognize that dynamic in CAPITALIST. It has a Brazilian board, festivals, Carnival, state buybacks and its own conquest objectives. CAPITALIST is independent and is not an official version of those brands.

How do I play CAPITALIST online with friends?

Open the game, choose Create room and share the code. Your friends open the same site and choose Join with code. A room holds 2–4 players in total, including the host. Everyone plays on their own internet-connected device, and the host starts the match.

Is the game free, with no download or sign-up?

Yes. Play CAPITALIST for free in your browser, without downloading an app or creating an account. The money used to buy properties and pay rent is fictional game money.

Can I play solo against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play with BOT in the menu, select your character and color, and start the match. The BOT is your computer-controlled opponent: it rolls dice, buys properties and builds by the same rules as you.

How long does a CAPITALIST match last?

The default duration is 30 minutes. In multiplayer, the host can set a limit of 30–99 minutes before starting. A match may end earlier if someone completes a conquest objective or is the last active player after bankruptcies.

Can I play on a phone, tablet or computer?

Yes. CAPITALIST runs in the browser on your computer, phone or tablet. On phones and tablets, play in landscape orientation; the game guides you into full screen when available. In multiplayer, everyone needs an internet connection.

How do I win in CAPITALIST?

Win by holding three complete groups on different sides, two complete groups on the same side, or all four beaches until each opponent completes a normal turn. You also win if you are the last active player. When time runs out, the highest property value wins; cash breaks ties. See examples in the victory rules.
